# User Guidelines: Smart Nutrition Tracker

Welcome to your personal nutrition assistant! Our app combines advanced AI image recognition with the comprehensive Indonesian Food Composition Database (TKPI) to help you accurately track your daily macros and micronutrients. 

Follow these guidelines to get the most out of your food logging experience.

---

## 1. Understanding Your Daily Goals

You don't need to guess your nutritional needs. The app calculates them for you automatically:
* **BMR & Macros:** Based on your weight, height, and activity level, the app calculates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) to set your daily calorie, protein, fat, and carbohydrate targets.
* **Micronutrients (AKG):** We use the Angka Kecukupan Gizi (AKG) standards based on your age to set highly specific daily targets for vitamins (A, B-complex, C, D, E, K) and minerals (Calcium, Iron, Zinc, etc.).

## 2. Adding Food via Label Scanner (OCR)

Want to log a packaged snack or drink? Let our AI do the heavy lifting by scanning the "Informasi Nilai Gizi" (Nutrition Facts) label.

### How to Scan
1. Tap the scanner icon and choose to either **take a new photo** or **upload an image from your gallery**.
2. Ensure the nutrition table is clearly visible, well-lit, and not cut off.
3. The AI will extract everything from basic calories to specific vitamins and minerals.

### Validating the Data
AI is smart, but camera blur happens! 
* **Unreadable Labels:** If the photo is too dark or blurry, the app will alert you that it couldn't extract the data. Please try taking a clearer photo.
* **Confirmation Page:** If the scan is successful, you will be taken to a confirmation page. **Always double-check the numbers.** If the AI misread an "8" as a "3", you can manually edit the values before saving the food to your log.

## 3. Searching the TKPI Database

For fresh foods, home-cooked meals, or ingredients without labels, use our built-in TKPI database.

### Searching & Selecting
* Simply type the food name into the search bar (e.g., "Nasi Putih", "Telur Ayam").
* **Pay Attention to State:** TKPI distinguishes between raw and cooked foods. Make sure you select the correct version (e.g., *Beras* vs. *Nasi*, or *Ayam Mentah* vs. *Ayam Goreng*) because cooking changes the nutritional volume and weight.

### Portion Sizes & Measurements
* **TKPI Foods (Solids):** The base measurement in TKPI is 100 grams (1 serving). You can adjust your intake using **Grams, Kilograms, or Servings** (e.g., entering "0.5 servings" will log 50g).
* **Scanned Liquids (OCR):** If you scanned a drink and the label uses milliliters (ml), the app will unlock volume-based measurements like **Cups, Liters, Tablespoons (tbsp), and Teaspoons (tsp)** for easier logging.

## 4. Tracking Your Progress

* **Daily Log:** All your scanned items and TKPI foods are saved into a single, easy-to-read daily list.
* **Analytics & Charts:** Navigate to the analytics page to see your daily intake charts. These charts show your progress toward your 100% daily goal for both macros and specific micronutrients. You can filter these charts to focus on specific nutrients you are trying to improve.
